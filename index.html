About

Sampadha Portal

Sampadha Portal is the client portal of Sampadha, a SEBI-registered Research Analyst (Corporate), registration number INH000031107. Clients use it to receive research, complete onboarding and manage their account. It is not a trading platform: Sampadha does not place orders or hold client funds or securities.

What the portal does

  • Delivers signed Cash and Multi research reports to subscribed clients.
  • Keeps client onboarding, risk profile, agreement and KYC records.
  • Shows each client their recommendations, positions and notifications.
  • With the client's consent, emails their Multi recommendations to their broker.

Why we ask for access to Gmail

Broker auto-email is optional. Brokers act on instructions from the email address a client has registered with them, so these emails need to come from the client's own Gmail. Only then, and only with the client's consent on Google's screen, the portal requests one permission: to send email on their behalf.

  • The portal cannot read, search or delete mail.
  • It sends only the order emails the client has consented to.
  • Google user data is never sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • The client can withdraw at any time, which stops sending and removes our access.
