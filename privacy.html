Sampadha Portal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The Sampadha Portal is used by Sampadha, a SEBI-registered Research Analyst (Corporate), registration number INH000031107, and its clients to deliver research reports, keep client onboarding records, and, where a client chooses, send order emails to the client's broker. This policy explains what we handle and why.

Information we handle

  • Identity and contact details: your name, date of birth, email address, mobile number, PAN, occupation, city and tax residency.
  • Financial and suitability details: annual income, net worth, investment experience, proposed investment amount, holding horizon, investment objective and loss tolerance.
  • The market segments, services and products you choose.
  • Broker and bank details for each product: broker name, depository, demat or BO ID, trading client ID, bank name, IFSC code and bank account number.
  • An onboarding PDF of these details, which is emailed to you for review and stored privately, and your acceptance of it.
  • Records required for our research services, such as KYC status, risk profile and signed agreements.
  • Your subscription, payment records, and the research reports delivered to you.
  • Broker auto-email details, entered by your Sampadha contact and shown to you before you consent: broker name, broker client ID and the order email addresses.
  • A log of emails sent through the portal and an audit record of important actions.

Connecting your Gmail

Broker auto-email is optional. If you choose it, you press Give consent on the Broker auto-email page and approve access once on Google's page, so that order emails reach your broker from your own email address. The portal asks Google for one permission only: to send email on your behalf.

  • We cannot read, search or delete your email. The permission we request allows sending only.
  • We use it only to send the Multi recommendations you receive in the portal to the broker addresses shown to you when you consent.
  • We also receive your Google email address, to check it matches your portal account.
  • The access token Google gives us is stored encrypted on our servers, is never shown in the portal, and is deleted when you disconnect.
  • Each email sent appears in your Gmail Sent folder.

Google user data

Sampadha Portal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Disconnecting

You can press Withdraw consent at any time on the Broker auto-email page in the portal, which stops sending immediately, removes our access and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • your broker, at the addresses shown to you, when you use broker auto-email;
  • service providers that host the portal and send email, and the Aadhaar eSign provider;
  • regulators and authorities, where the law requires it, including research filings.

We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping information

We keep records for as long as your account is active and for as long as we must to meet legal and regulatory record-keeping duties. Gmail access tokens are deleted when you disconnect.

Security

Access is limited by role, sensitive actions are logged, and information is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or correct your information, or withdraw an optional consent, by writing to the address below. Some records must be kept by law.
